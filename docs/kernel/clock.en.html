Clock and scheduling

Clock & Scheduling

The audio output is the master clock. The rendering target of the video frame is vsync quantized and aligned to the audio clock, and there is no independent display clock between audio and video. Explicitly enter buffering when buffer is low, clock freezes and re-anchors after data recovery.

  • master clock Audio
  • ring buffer 192,000 frames
  • vsync Show target quantification

audio master clockオーディオマスター#

All audio output backends push data into unified f32 PCM ring buffer (capacity 192,000 frames, oldest on overflow). The playback clock is based on the media time and derives the current time by combining the anchor moment and double speed.

Presenter every time render_tick Read the audio clock snapshot and synchronize it to the playback thread. Clock correction is governed by three parameters:

parametersDefaultmeaning
deadband5 msJitters with deviations smaller than this value are not corrected.
Maximum single correction5 msThe upper limit of each correction to avoid jumps.
snapshot threshold250 msDeviations exceeding this value are considered derailing and are re-anchored directly.

The reference time is the media time plus the queued audio duration (audio lead 120 ms), so that the video is presented at the end of the audio queue in advance. When the multiplier is not 1, audio clock synchronization is skipped, and the audio speed is changed by SoundTouch (SEQUENCE 25 ms).

vsync Quantitative SchedulingVSync スケジューリング#

The rendering targets of video frames are quantized at vsync intervals (default 16,666,667 ns, which is 60 Hz). The scheduler makes one of three decisions for each frame:

  • Present — The target time falls within this vsync cycle and is presented immediately.
  • Wait — The frame is earlier than the rendering window, waiting for the next cycle.
  • Drop — Frames later than the rendering window are skipped to reduce cumulative latency.

The upper limit of continuous frame loss is 5 frames. After reaching the upper limit, stop losing frames and return to Wait to avoid no picture output for a long time.

While the clock is frozen during the pause, seek decodes and renders the keyframe at the requested position while keeping the media clock from advancing.

buffer statusバッファリング#

When the audio or video input is insufficient, playback enters explicit buffering: the playback clock freezes and no longer advances; after the data is restored, the clock is re-anchored according to the restored media time. host meridian BufferingChanged Event aware state changes.

The clock is not advanced during buffering to avoid long frame drops or black screens after recovery.
